# 🏰 Wizard's Chess 3D — Gothic Cathedral Edition

An immersive 3D chess game rendered in a detailed Gothic Cathedral sanctum with real-time dynamic lighting, procedural marble & obsidian pieces, full chess rule validation, particle effects, and AI automaton mode.

![Wizard's Chess 3D](https://img.shields.io/badge/Three.js-r128-black?logo=three.js)
![Chess.js](https://img.shields.io/badge/Rules-Chess.js-green)
![TailwindCSS](https://img.shields.io/badge/TailwindCSS-v3-38bdf8?logo=tailwindcss)

## 🎮 Features

- **Gothic Cathedral Environment**: Stained glass windows with light shafts, gothic arches, stone pillars, burning torch sconces, and floating dust motes.
- **Procedural 3D Pieces**: Procedurally modeled King, Queen, Bishop, Knight, Rook, and Pawn pieces with realistic obsidian and white marble textures.
- **Rule Enforcement**: Powered by `Chess.js` (complete with check, checkmate, stalemate, castling, en passant, and pawn promotion).
- **Multiple Game Modes**:
  - **Local Solo / Pass-and-Play**: Play both sides locally.
  - **vs Automaton**: Play against the built-in chess AI.
  - **Cathedral Orbit**: Cinematic auto-rotation camera around the sanctum.
- **Sound & VFX**: Dynamic piece movements, piece shattering animations, and checkmate alerts.

## 🕹️ Controls

- **Orbit Camera**: Left Click + Drag
- **Pan Camera**: Right Click + Drag
- **Zoom**: Mouse Scroll Wheel
- **Select & Move**: Click on any piece to see valid moves highlighted, then click a highlighted destination tile.
